# Prompt: Build a Modern Company Website for Ibrahimawa Global and Farm (IGAF) Limited

Create a premium, responsive, and professional company website for **IBRAHIMAWA GLOBAL AND FARM (IGAF) LIMITED**, an agricultural equipment and machinery company in Nigeria.

The website should inspire trust, look modern, and feel like a real corporate business. Use a clean design with a green, white, and gold color palette that reflects agriculture, sustainability, and professionalism.

## Design Style

* Modern and elegant
* Mobile-first responsive design
* Smooth scrolling and subtle animations
* Large hero section with high-quality agricultural imagery
* Rounded cards and soft shadows
* Professional typography
* Fast loading
* Accessible design
* Clean navigation bar
* Sticky header
* Footer with company information

## Navigation

* Home
* About Us
* Products
* Why Choose Us
* Gallery
* Contact

## Hero Section

Headline:

**Empowering Modern Agriculture with Quality Farm Machinery**

Subheading:

"We supply reliable agricultural machines and equipment that help farmers increase productivity, reduce manual labor, and grow successful businesses."

Buttons:

* View Products
* Contact Us

Background:

A beautiful rice farm with modern agricultural machinery working in the field.

## About Section

Title:

**About Ibrahimawa Global and Farm (IGAF) Limited**

Content:

Introduce the company as a trusted supplier of agricultural machinery dedicated to supporting farmers with durable, efficient, and affordable farming equipment. Emphasize quality, customer satisfaction, innovation, and helping improve agricultural productivity across Nigeria.

Display products as beautiful cards with realistic images, icons, and short descriptions.

* Multipurpose Cleaner

* Rice Reaper (correct the spelling if "Rice Riffer" was intended to mean Rice Reaper)

Each card should contain:

* Large image
* Product name
* Short description
* "Contact for Enquiry" button

No prices.

## Why Choose Us

Show feature cards with icons.

Display a responsive grid of farming machinery, rice processing, tractors, tillers, farmers using equipment, irrigation systems, and agricultural fields.
